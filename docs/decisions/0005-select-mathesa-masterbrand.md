# ADR-0005: Select Mathesa as the replacement masterbrand

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-08-01

## Decision

`Mathesa` is selected as the product/masterbrand replacement for the `Veristat`
working name. This closes candidate selection for the rebrand. It does not
represent trademark clearance or a claim that domains, handles, packages, or
other identifiers are available.

## Parked work

Expanded trademark and common-law clearance, domain and handle acquisition,
pronunciation testing, and outside-counsel review are parked by owner direction.
They may be resumed before an irreversible public-launch decision, but are not
prerequisites to recording the name or preparing a reversible migration plan.

## Boundaries

This decision authorizes documentation of the name and reversible human-facing
rebrand preparation only. It does not authorize renaming `consensus_check`,
`get_sample_verdict`, `VeristatMCP`, D1, the Worker or its URL, migrations,
historical evidence, payment/runtime identifiers, or the repository. It also
does not authorize deployment, publication, registry action, visibility change,
purchase, registration, trademark filing, paid call, or external contact. Those
actions retain their existing migration, release, and owner gates.

The provider-blind Round 1 record remains preserved historical evidence. Its
no-selection result is not retroactively changed; Mathesa was selected during
the subsequent owner-led exploration.
